Owner Corporation Strata Cleaning you can rely on

Well-kept common areas protect property values and keep residents happy.

Daynamic looks after the common areas of residential and mixed-use buildings for owner corporations and strata managers across Melbourne. Lobbies, corridors, lifts, stairwells, car parks and outdoor areas are cleaned on a regular schedule agreed with the committee or building manager.

Alongside routine cleaning we handle periodic work such as carpet steam cleaning, pressure washing and window cleaning, so you have one accountable contractor for the whole building.

Frequently asked questions

What does strata cleaning include?

Strata cleaning covers shared areas such as entrances, lobbies, corridors, lifts, stairwells, bin rooms and car parks. We tailor the scope and frequency to each building.

Can you work with our strata manager?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with strata and building managers on access, scope changes and reporting so the committee does not have to manage the day-to-day.

Do you offer periodic deep cleans?

Yes. Carpet steam cleaning, pressure washing, window cleaning and other periodic services can be scheduled alongside your regular clean.
